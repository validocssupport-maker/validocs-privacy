The short version

ExpatWay does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no ExpatWay server.

Everything you put into ExpatWay — your documents, their photos, your family members, your notes, your Ibadah record, your settings — is stored only on your own phone. None of it is transmitted to Falah Labs or to anyone else. We could not read it if we wanted to, because we never receive it.

The only way any of your data leaves your phone is if you export a backup file and choose where to put it.

What Information We Collect

We collect nothing.

ExpatWay has no user accounts, no registration, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no backend server of its own. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, rent or share:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier
  • your documents, document numbers, expiry dates or document photographs
  • your family or team members' details
  • your notes, tasks, calculations, favourites or bookmarks
  • your Ibadah Tracker record
  • your location
  • your device identifiers, advertising ID, contacts, call log, SMS or installed-app list

Earlier versions of ExpatWay (up to 1.0.3) offered an optional account with cloud backup. That feature has been removed completely. There are no ExpatWay user accounts, and the databases that supported them have been deleted.

How We Use Your Information

We do not have your information, so there is nothing for us to use it for.

Everything the app does with the data you enter happens on your device: calculating expiry status, scheduling reminders, working out prayer times, adding up a dependant fee, and drawing your Ibadah month.

Local Storage

All app data is written to your device's private application storage, which other apps cannot read.

This includes:

  • Family and team members, including profile pictures
  • Documents, expiry dates, custom alert dates and document photographs
  • Notes and task lists, including due dates and reminders
  • Saved dependant-fee calculations
  • Starred government services and bookmarked news articles
  • Your Ibadah Tracker record
  • Which reminders you have switched on
  • Your event-alert preference and the filters it uses
  • Your theme (dark/light) and calendar (Gregorian/Hijri) preference
  • Small caches so the app works offline: recent weather, your city name, currency rates, cached news headlines and the events list

Uninstalling ExpatWay deletes all of it. You can also erase everything at any time from Settings ▸ Backup & Restore ▸ Erase all app data. That action is immediate, permanent and cannot be undone by us, because we hold no copy.

Backup — A File That Belongs To You

ExpatWay's backup is a file you create and control, not a cloud service we operate.

  • Tapping Back up now produces a single .ewbak file containing your app data, including your document photographs and profile pictures.
  • You choose where the file goes: your own Google Drive, a folder on your phone or SD card, or another app such as OneDrive, Dropbox, WhatsApp or email via the share sheet.
  • The file is never sent to Falah Labs and never passes through any server we control.
  • You may optionally protect the file with a password. It is then encrypted with AES-256, using a key derived from your password with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a random salt. There is no account and no password reset. If you forget the password, that file cannot be opened again — by us or by anyone.
  • Once the file is in your Google Drive, OneDrive, phone storage or a chat, it is governed by that service's privacy policy and your own settings there, not by this one.

Restoring reads a file you select and replaces the data on your phone with its contents.

Google Sign-In — Used Only For Google Drive

If, and only if, you choose to back up to your own Google Drive, ExpatWay asks you to sign in with Google.

This does not create an ExpatWay account and is not used to identify you. It exists solely to obtain your permission to write a file to your Drive.
  • The scope requested is drive.file only. This grants access exclusively to files ExpatWay itself creates. ExpatWay cannot see, read, list or modify any other file, folder, photo or document in your Google Drive.
  • Backups are placed in a folder named ExpatWay Backups in your Drive. They are your files, in your account, under your control.
  • Your Google account email is stored on your device only, so the app can show you which account is connected. It is not transmitted anywhere.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Settings ▸ Backup & Restore ▸ Back up now ▸ Disconnect, and revoke access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete backups already in your Drive — they remain yours.

If you never use Google Drive backup, ExpatWay never asks you to sign in to anything.

Location

ExpatWay requests foreground location (while you are using the app) for exactly three purposes:

  • Prayer times — calculating accurate Umm al-Qura prayer times for where you actually are
  • Qibla — working out the direction of the Kaaba from your position
  • Prayer-anchored reminders — Azkar, Surah Al-Mulk and the Ibadah Tracker nudge, which fire relative to Fajr, Asr or Isha, and the local weather and city name shown at the top of the home screen

What happens to it:

  • Prayer times, Qibla direction and reminder times are calculated entirely on your device, using an offline astronomical library. No location is sent anywhere for these.
  • Your last-known coordinates are cached in the app's private storage so reminders can be re-armed without asking for your position again. They are not uploaded.
  • Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) solely to fetch the current temperature for the home-screen weather chip. This request carries no identifier of any kind — no name, no account, no device ID.
  • Your coordinates are passed to your device's built-in geocoder (part of Android / Google Play services) to turn them into a city name such as "Riyadh". That is an operating-system service, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • ExpatWay never tracks your movements, never records a location history, and never uses location in the background.

Location is optional. Decline it and everything else in the app works normally; only prayer times, Qibla, the weather chip and the prayer-anchored reminders are unavailable.

Notifications

All notifications are local — scheduled by the app on your own device. ExpatWay has no push server and cannot send you anything from the outside.

Notifications you may opt into:

  • Document expiry alerts, timed per document type, plus a 7-day and a same-day warning
  • Custom alert dates you set on an individual document
  • Due dates on tasks in Notes & Tasks
  • Religion reminders: prayer times, Morning and Evening Azkar, Surah Al-Mulk, Friday Surah Al-Kahf, and the Ibadah Tracker nudge
  • Event alerts for events matching the filters you choose on the Events screen

Every one of these is off until you turn it on, and each can be turned off individually. Notification content is generated on your device from your own data and is never transmitted.

Camera, Photos and Files

  • Camera — used only when you take a photo of a document, or open the QR / barcode scanner in Expat Tools. Photos are saved to the app's private storage. Scanned codes are shown to you and are never uploaded; a scanned web address is always displayed in full for you to confirm before anything opens.
  • Photos / media — used only when you pick an existing image for a document or profile picture, and when you save an Ibadah month report or a generated PDF to your device.
  • Files — used only when you choose where to save a backup, or select a backup file to restore.

ExpatWay does not scan, index or upload your photo library. It accesses only the specific items you select.

ExpatWay does not record audio. A microphone permission is declared by the standard camera component the app is built on; ExpatWay never requests it and never uses it.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

ExpatWay is a native mobile application. It uses no cookies, no web beacons, no pixels, no SDK-based analytics, no advertising identifiers and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. There is no advertising in the app.

Data Sharing

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. We do not sell, rent, trade or disclose your personal information to anyone — not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to partners.

The only parties involved at all are:

  • Google Play Services / Google Drive API — only if you choose Google Drive backup, and only for files ExpatWay itself created. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Console — Google provides Falah Labs with aggregate, anonymous statistics about installs, uninstalls, ratings and crashes as part of distributing on the Play Store. This is Google's standard developer reporting; it is not collected by ExpatWay, contains no personal data, and cannot be linked to an individual user by us.

International Data Transfers

ExpatWay does not transfer your personal data anywhere, because it does not send your personal data anywhere. Your data stays on your device unless you export a backup yourself. If you choose to store that backup in a cloud service, that service decides where the file is held — please see its own policy.

Data Retention

Falah Labs retains no user data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete.

Your own data is retained on your phone for exactly as long as you keep it there. It is removed when you delete the item, use Erase all app data, or uninstall the app.

Backup files you have created are retained wherever you put them, until you delete them.

Opt-Out and Data Deletion

There is no data collection to opt out of and no account to delete.

To remove your data:

  • Open Settings ▸ Backup & Restore ▸ Erase all app data, and confirm; or
  • Uninstall ExpatWay from your device.

Either removes every member, document, photograph, note, saved calculation, Ibadah record and setting from the phone. It is permanent — there is no server copy for us to restore from. Any backup file you made is separate, and you delete it yourself from wherever you saved it.

To revoke Google Drive access, use Disconnect in the app, and/or remove ExpatWay at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ExpatWay is intended for adults managing residency and family documents in Saudi Arabia. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed for or marketed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If a parent or guardian uses ExpatWay to track a child's documents, that information stays on the parent's own device and is never transmitted to us.

Security

  • All data is held in your device's private application storage, which the Android sandbox prevents other apps from reading.
  • Backup files may be encrypted with AES-256 using a password you choose, with the key derived via PBKDF2-SHA256 and a random per-file salt.
  • Google Drive uploads use HTTPS, with the narrowest available scope (drive.file).
  • Every network request the app makes uses HTTPS.
  • There is no server to breach, no user database, and no credentials of yours for anyone to steal from us — which is the strongest security property this app has.

The security of your device itself — its lock screen, its encryption, who has physical access to it, and where you put your backup files — remains your responsibility.

Data Breach Notification

Falah Labs operates no server and holds no user data, so a breach of our systems could not expose your personal information.

In the unlikely event that we became aware of a security issue in the app itself that could put user data at risk, we would publish a notice on this page and release a corrective update through the Google Play Store as quickly as possible, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of it where a notification obligation applies.
